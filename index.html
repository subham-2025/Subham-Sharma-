Social RAG Query Intent Classifier

Defined the labeling taxonomy and iterated guidelines through model error analysis for Facebook's query-intent classifier on Llama 3.3 (70B). The taxonomy had to hold up across ambiguous, multi-intent queries — the kind that break most rule-based routing.

LLM-as-Judge Alignment Study

Built a 700-sample stratified eval, taxonomized failures into binary pass/fail rubrics, and refined judge prompts through false-positive/false-negative analysis. Most of the gain came from finding where the judge and human raters disagreed for structural reasons, not just noisy labels.

92.4% human–AI alignment
